Who we are

We are Bengali Hindus, united across the globe by our shared heritage, culture, and values. Through this platform, we have come together as a worldwide community-spanning continents and countries-to amplify our collective voice and support one another, no matter where we live.

Our Mission

Our primary aim is to raise global awareness about the ongoing atrocities and discrimination faced by Hindus in Bangladesh, particularly under the current government. Despite being one of the country’s oldest and most integral communities, Bengali Hindus in Bangladesh continue to face violence, social exclusion, and systemic deprivation in various sectors, including education, employment, and public service.

We seek the attention of the international community to help restore and protect the basic human rights of Hindus in Bangladesh. Our goal is to ensure that every individual, regardless of their faith, can live with dignity, security, and equal opportunity.

We want to make it clear that our movement is not against any specific community or religion. We stand for peace, coexistence, and mutual respect. Our struggle is solely for our right to live peacefully, to be treated fairly, and to enjoy the same freedoms and protections as any other citizen. We believe in building bridges, not walls, and we invite all who share our vision of justice and equality to join us in this cause.
