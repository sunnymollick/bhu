About the Temple

Dhakeshwari Temple (Bengali: ঢাকেশ্বরী মন্দির) is a state-owned Hindu temple in Dhaka, Bangladesh. It is the national temple of Bangladesh. The name "Dhakeshwari" means "Goddess of Dhaka". Since the destruction of Ramna Kali Mandir in 1971 by the Pakistan Army during the Bangladesh Liberation War, the Dhakeshwari Temple has assumed status as the most important Hindu place of worship in Bangladesh.

The temple's history is intertwined with the history of Dhaka itself. It is widely believed that the city was named after the Goddess Dhakeshwari. The main deity is a ten-armed goddess, a form of Durga, which is a significant figure in the Shakta tradition of Hinduism. The temple complex has undergone several renovations and additions over the centuries, reflecting the changing architectural styles and the patronage of various rulers and devotees.
